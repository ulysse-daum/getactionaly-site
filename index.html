Actionaly

getactionaly.com is an outreach domain operated by Actionaly. Our main website is actionaly.com.

Actionaly is a Family Relationship Management (FRM) platform for K-12 school districts: one place for messaging families, translating automatically, and collecting responses. Our standalone consent package handles parental consent end to end, from the request sent to the parent's phone through reminders and a live record staff can check for any student.

If you received an email from an @getactionaly.com address, it came from our team. Replying to that email is the fastest way to reach us. You can also visit actionaly.com to learn more.
