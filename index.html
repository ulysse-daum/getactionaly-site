Actionaly

getactionaly.com is a domain Actionaly uses to send outreach email to school districts. Our website is actionaly.com.

Actionaly helps K-12 districts collect parental consent — sending the request to a parent's phone, chasing the families who have not replied, and keeping a record staff can check for any student.

We send introductory email from this domain rather than our main one — it is common practice, and it keeps outreach separate from the mail our customers rely on.

If you want to confirm an email you received is really from us, do not take this page's word for it. Go to actionaly.com/verify — you can type that address in yourself — or write to support@actionaly.com with the sender's address and we will confirm whether it came from us.
